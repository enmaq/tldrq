# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes respectful, constructive participation from everyone.

## Our standards

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate, even when disagreeing.
- Focus on ideas and work, not on individuals.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and unwelcome behaviour.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory language, and repeated disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **support@enmaq.com**. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible, and may result in moderation, removal of content, or restriction of participation.

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and community discussions.
